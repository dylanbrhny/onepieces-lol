The daily voyage

Name a character. The rarer the right answer, the further your ship sails.

? How to play

7 prompts a day, 30 seconds each. Most ask for a character; a couple ask for a place, Devil Fruit, ship or crew. Every right answer sails at least 100 nmi: famous picks sail a little, deep cuts up to 1,000. A wrong answer is turned away so you can try again; you only score 0 if time runs out. The route is 7,000 nmi, so only seven Pirate King answers reach the end.

    Unofficial fan game. Not affiliated with Eiichiro Oda, Shueisha or Toei Animation. Character data and pictures from the One Piece Wiki (text under CC BY-SA 3.0). Your answers are counted anonymously to rank how common they are.
